The Recruitment Process

Finding the right person, not just filling a vacancy.

We start by understanding the business behind the role: how the company works, where the pressure points are, what the team needs, and what success really looks like. That context shapes the search, the conversations, and the shortlist.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    A focused conversation about the business, role, pain points, team, urgency, and non-negotiables.

  2. 2

    Role and candidate profile

    Clarify the responsibilities, experience, working style, values, and practical fit needed to succeed.

  3. 3

    Search strategy

    Build a targeted approach around the role, the market, and the kind of candidate who will add real value.

  4. 4

    Headhunting and outreach

    Thoughtfully identify and approach relevant candidates, including strong people who may not be actively looking.

  5. 5

    Shortlist and insight

    Present a considered shortlist with context, communicate clearly, and share useful market insight.

  6. 6

    Interview to onboarding

    Coordinate interviews and communication, manage reference-check administration, and support a smooth handover.
